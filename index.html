Chapter · I

CH · I · The Lone Path
— Synopsis

Honor is a road,
and his is burning.

Once sworn to a daimyō who no longer exists, Kaito Mori walks west with a borrowed name and a katana that won't stay sheathed. Six provinces, three rival clans, and the slow-burning memory of a village where the rain still smells like smoke.

Choose vengeance and the world will fear you. Choose honor and it will hollow you. The road remembers either way.

"The blade does not ask the wind which way to fall. It only asks who is left standing." — Kaito Mori, Ch. III
